# Decisions

- 2026-10-08: Verbatim means the logic, not the layout: ruff format is allowed on handed-over files (hook.py and hook_chart.py reformatted, layout only). docs/ is excluded from ruff so briefs are never reformatted.
- 2026-10-08: OpenAQ archive stamps each reading at the END of its period (T4: station 235 Anand Vihar, 2025-11-12, 15-min periods; 84/84 rows equal the API period ending at the stamp, 56/76 the one starting there). STAMPED_AT_END = True.
- 2026-10-08: T4 lines rows up by timestamp and compares values instead of matching unique values: CPCB repeats each hourly value across its four 15-min slots, so only 1 of 84 values was unique.
- 2026-10-08: to_hourly floors on the IST clock, not UTC. Archive data is 15-minute, and with UTC flooring the "08" bin held 08:30-09:30 IST because IST is UTC+5:30. hour_utc is still stored in UTC (now at :30); a 15-minute test covers it.
- 2026-10-08: Train and evaluate on 2025-26 only, walk-forward by week from 10 Nov 2025 to 30 Jan 2026 (architect, 01:10). OpenAQ has no Delhi CPCB data from about 2020 to Jan 2025: stations with >= 60% coverage were 1 (US Embassy 8118) in each of 2022-23, 2023-24 and 2024-25, and 47 in 2025-26.
- 2026-10-08: The US Embassy monitor 8118 (AirNow, hourly) stamps the START of its period, unlike CPCB (2023-11-14: 23/23 rows equal the API period starting at the stamp, 0/22 the one ending there). The pull still bins every station with STAMPED_AT_END = True, so 8118 sits one hour early in pm25_hourly.parquet; use its own verdict wherever 8118 is used.
- 2026-10-08: Timestamp convention is set per provider in scripts/config.py: CPCB END, caaqm END (station 7044, 2022-10-12, 16/16 rows), AirNow START. Stations of an unchecked provider are skipped with a warning, never defaulted (StateAir, which has no data in our months).
- 2026-10-08: The pull bins all of a station's months in one to_hourly call, not per station-month: with 8118 on START, the 1 Nov 2023 00:00 hour had readings in both the October and November files. pull_archive.py --cached rebuilds from data/raw without listing S3.
- 2026-10-08: From 1 Oct to 3 Nov 2023, 8118 carries two interleaved series (stamps at :00 and :30 IST, one sensor id); its hours there average both. Its -999 missing-value rows are dropped by to_hourly.
- 2026-10-08: hook_chart puts the cleanest-hour label below-left of its marker: on the real Nov-Dec 2025 profile the evening rise ran through it (layout only).
- 2026-10-08: T6 day-level stats take every weekday station-day with all six school hours, with no station coverage filter (48 stations, 1,541 station-days).
- 2026-10-08: T6 item 3: 8118 has 11:00 IST readings on only 2 days of Nov-Dec 2023, so its 2023 cleanest hour is taken over the other five school hours (the cut is a lower bound); hook_stat.json records it.
- 2026-10-08: The monitoring data is labelled "Delhi NCR" (7 of the 48 stations in the 2025 profile are in Gurugram, Noida, Ghaziabad and Bahadurgarh; GRAP school orders cover those districts too). Schools stay "Delhi schools".
- 2026-10-08: meteo.fetch floors its IST index to the hour. Open-Meteo hours are UTC, so :30 IST, and build_rows looks met up at IST :00 hours: as handed over, every weather and CAMS feature was NaN on real data (100% -> 0% after the fix). Each value sits mid-hour, so it labels the IST hour it falls in. A test covers fetch -> build_rows.
- 2026-10-08: features.build_rows raises ValueError when met's index or hourly["hour_ist"] has a timestamp off the hour (minute != 0), so a misaligned input crashes instead of becoming NaN features. Checked after the empty-input return, which keeps the Forecast Lambda's no-readings error. One test per input.
- 2026-10-08: One model for every school hour: no persistence splice or blend at short leads (architect). In the winter backtest the model trails persistence by 0.8-2.1 µg/m³ at leads 4-6 h, noise next to a ~50 MAE, and persistence is flat across the six hours, so it cannot rank them. MAE by lead in hours, model/persistence: 4 42.8/42.0 · 5 47.6/46.1 · 6 53.5/51.4 · 7 52.7/58.9 · 8 49.7/72.6 · 9 47.8/85.3 · 10 62.1/89.7 · 11 64.7/102.0 · 12 63.2/85.9 · 13 50.0/106.8 · 14 57.6/102.8 · 15 44.1/55.0.
- 2026-10-08: The final model trains through the last full day: the day before the newest PM2.5 hour unless that hour is 23:00. The parquet ends 2026-10-02 13:00 IST, so trained_through is 2026-10-01. models/*.txt is gitignored; features.json is committed.
- 2026-10-08: T7 pilots: stations are the 46 model stations (still reporting, >= 60% coverage in 2025-26), so every pilot maps to a station the Lambdas forecast; schools without a name are left out (the alert names the school); the spread starts from the school closest to its station. schools.csv is cached: delete it to query Overpass again.
- 2026-10-08: The assembly call and the "fine" check use the latest reading (the 04:00 IST bin, blended like the forecasts); the Clear Hour stays with the model. Winter backtest: the latest reading called "hold assembly indoors" at 0.887 accuracy vs 0.862 for the 08:00 forecast (misses 107 vs 126, needless calls 153 vs 191), and of 129 days the model called fine only 48 were.
- 2026-10-08: With assembly held indoors, 08:00 is never the Clear Hour.
- 2026-10-08: The advice adds "Keep outdoor time short." when even the Clear Hour is forecast above 120 µg/m³ (CPCB "very poor"); in winter the chosen hour averaged 151 µg/m³.
- 2026-10-08: Pilots name a specific school: a name made only of school-type words (ignoring digits and single letters) drops out. The word list is the architect's plus "coeducational" and "educational", the long forms of co/ed/coed, which otherwise let "Government Coeducational Senior Secondary School" through.
- 2026-10-08: A pilot's nearest model station must be inside Delhi, judged by station name (Noida, Gurugram/Gurgaon, Ghaziabad, Faridabad, Bahadurgarh are out). Owner alone is not enough: NISE Gwal Pahari (10921) is an IMD station in Gurugram.
- 2026-10-08: Correction to the Delhi NCR line above: 8 of the 48 stations in the 2025 profile are outside Delhi, not 7. NISE Gwal Pahari was missed by counting by owner. The label already covers it.
- 2026-10-08: A .dockerignore keeps .git, .venv, .aws-sam, data, outputs, models, docs and web out of the Forecast image's build context: SAM builds from the repo root, and the image copies only src/clearhour and its requirements.txt.
- 2026-10-08: deploy.sh passes --no-fail-on-empty-changeset, so a re-run that only re-uploads the model or the archive doesn't fail on an unchanged stack. samconfig.toml is written by hand and holds no parameter values.
